A Canon of Science Fiction

No book under the cursor

The books that built the genre, from Frankenstein forward, colored by era. A click on the band of a decade enters it, and a book under the cursor shows its cover. Ctrl (or ⌘) with the wheel zooms; the wheel alone scrolls inside the frame and dragging pans the view.

Covers come from Open Library and are loaded from its servers for identification only. The list is editorial: the books here are the ones that defined what science fiction could do, not a complete history.
