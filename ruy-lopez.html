The Ruy Lopez

Every branch of the Ruy Lopez from move 3 to move 9, following the Closed main line outward. Arc size is how often the line is played among 179,626 Ruy Lopez games. Click an arc to zoom into that line, click the center to back out, hover to see the position.

The tree is thirteen plies deep and rings stop where a line was not followed further; gaps are moves below the cut. Counts are from the 365chess.com Big Database, 3,902,072 games, accessed August 2026. Percentages are the share of the Ruy Lopez.
