The Universe in Time

A mark under the cursor lands here

Time runs left to right from the Big Bang, on four scales. Back from now counts years before the present, so each step right is a tenth of the time left. Forward from the Big Bang counts years since it. Sun at the center is the same count stretched so the Sun sits on the middle, giving the nine billion years before it half the line. The even scale is one rate throughout, and shows what the logarithms hide.

The named milestones above the line carry their dating and its source, and where a claim is disputed the card says so and gives the firmer date beside it. Bars are the published uncertainty. The strands below the line hold the detail, from a Big History notebook; every date in them was checked against the current literature, and the ones that had drifted are corrected and listed under the references.

What the notebook said, and what the line says now
The cosmic dark agesThe notebook: began 13.796 Ga, before the Big Bang itself. The line now: the dark ages run from recombination to reionization, and end 13.4 Ga.
The giant planetsThe notebook: 4.59 Ga, before the Sun. The line now: Jupiter's core grew within a few million years of the first solids, 4.564 Ga.
The MoonThe notebook: 4.53 Ga. The line now: the giant impact is now dated near 4.51 Ga.
The earliest known galaxiesThe notebook: 13.407 Ga. The line now: MoM z14, announced in 2025, sits at 13.52 Ga.
The Permian extinctionThe notebook: an interval of 250 to 200 Ma. The line now: it is a short event at 251.9 Ma.
The Chicxulub impactThe notebook: 65 Ma. The line now: 66.0 Ma.
The first humanThe notebook: Homo sapiens at 2.8 Ma. The line now: 2.8 Ma is the earliest jaw assigned to the genus Homo.
Acheulean handaxesThe notebook: 2.8 Ma. The line now: 1.76 Ma at Kokiselei.
Homo sapiensThe notebook: 200 ka. The line now: 315 ka at Jebel Irhoud.
Twisted plant fibreThe notebook: 90 ka. The line now: the Neanderthal cord from Abri du Maras is 50 ka.
AustraliaThe notebook: settled 55 ka. The line now: 65 ka at Madjedbebe.
The domestic dogThe notebook: 30 ka. The line now: the oldest undisputed dog burial is 14.2 ka, and the date is contested.
Pigs in New GuineaThe notebook: 7000 BCE. The line now: they arrive within the last few thousand years, so the mark was dropped.
The dromedaryThe notebook: 3000 BCE. The line now: about 1000 BCE.
AdwaThe notebook: an Ethiopian victory in 1340. The line now: Adwa was fought in 1896, and the mark was dropped.
The Inca empireThe notebook: 1200. The line now: the kingdom of Cusco is that old, the empire expands from 1438.
AngkorThe notebook: abandoned about 1400. The line now: 1431.
A world population of one billionThe notebook: 1750. The line now: about 1800.
A world population of two billionThe notebook: 1900. The line now: 1927.
