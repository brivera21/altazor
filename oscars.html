Best Picture Winners

Every Academy Award Best Picture winner, from Wings at the first ceremony in 1929 to Anora in 2025, grouped into eight Hollywood eras. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click an era band to zoom into it, hover a film to see its poster.

Years are ceremony years. Winners and eras follow a Mathematica timeline notebook, with Anora appended. Posters are loaded at view time from Wikipedia's public API for identification and are not stored on this site; a few may fail to resolve.
