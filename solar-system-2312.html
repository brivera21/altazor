The Solar System of 2312

A world under the cursor lands here

The worlds sit on one line by distance from the Sun, logarithmic so that Mercury and Pluto share it, and the disks run on a separate logarithmic size scale. Nothing here is a picture of the sky. Amber marks where Kim Stanley Robinson's 2312 puts its story, and each card pairs what the book says with what is actually there.

The violet arcs are Swan Er Hong's travel through the novel in order, following the synopsis on the author's own site, and each stop draws the world she lands on to scale, with the novel's structures in amber. Pluto is on the map and off her route: that is where the qubes are gathered at the end.
