title: Chess
---

[Openings by Frequency](openings.html). The opening tree of 3.9 million games as an interactive sunburst. Click an arc to zoom into a line, hover to see the position.

[The Ruy Lopez](ruy-lopez.html). Every branch of the Ruy Lopez from move 3 to move 9, thirteen plies deep, with the named variations.

[50 Greatest Games](timeline.html). A zoomable timeline of the fifty games worth knowing, the world champions, and their eras, from 950 to 2024.

[Board Intuition](intuition.html). The geography of the chessboard: the center, the files, mobility heatmaps, outposts, and the endgame rules, each painted onto the same reference board.

[Endgames](endgames.html). King and pawn against king, then the basic checkmates, played out move by move: the queen, the rook, and the two bishops, in the open and into the corner. Every line is checked against a solved table of its ending, and one board paints every placement in that table at once.
