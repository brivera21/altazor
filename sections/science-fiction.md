## Science Fiction Concepts

[O'Neill Ring](oneill-ring.html). A rotating habitat seen along its axis and from inside: radius and spin set the artificial gravity, rim speed and comfort zone, and a dropped ball traces the Coriolis curve.

[Generation Starship](generation-starship.html). A star, a cruise speed and an acceleration phase give the generations born aboard, the energy the trip costs, the minimum crew, and whether waiting for faster ships would beat leaving now.

[Space Elevator](space-elevator.html). A cable from the equator to a counterweight at 100,000 km, drawn to scale with the Earth: apparent gravity along the ride, the climb time at a chosen speed, and the exact orbit of anything released along the way.

[The Mars of Red Mars](red-mars.html). The real planet under Kim Stanley Robinson's novel: the canyons at their USGS coordinates on a MOLA-shaded globe, the book's places marked where the text puts them, and a card for each saying what the novel gets right.
