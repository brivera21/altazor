50 Greatest Games

The fifty games worth knowing, the world champions, and the eras they belong to, from As-Suli in 950 to Gukesh in 2024. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, click an era band to zoom into it, hover anything for details.

Games are placed at the year they were played and colored by era; champions sit below the axis at the start of each reign. The game list is a personal selection. The axis starts at 1800; the medieval and early games live in the Before 1800 box so a thousand mostly empty years do not stretch the canvas.
