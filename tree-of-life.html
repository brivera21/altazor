Tree of Life

A group under the cursor lands here

The tree runs from the last universal common ancestor at the left to living groups at the right; branch lengths carry no time information. The eukaryotes are drawn where current evidence places them, beside the Asgard archaea inside the archaeal branch, which turns Woese's three domains into two. A node under the cursor fills the card; a click pins it, and a second click lets go. Each living tip carries the photograph from its group's Wikipedia article, fetched at view time.
