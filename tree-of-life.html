Tree of Life

A group under the cursor lands here

The tree runs from the last universal common ancestor at the left to living groups at the right; branch lengths carry no time information. The eukaryotes are drawn where current evidence places them, beside the Asgard archaea inside the archaeal branch, which turns Woese's three domains into two; the cards say what each group is.
