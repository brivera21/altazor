Openings by Frequency

The opening tree of 3.9 million games. Ring position is the move number, arc size is how often the line is played. Click an arc to zoom into that line, click the center to back out, hover to see the position.

Arcs cover only the lines above the frequency cutoff of the 394-node tree; the gaps are everything rarer. Counts are from the 365chess.com Big Database, 3,902,072 games, accessed August 2026. Percentages are the share of all games.
