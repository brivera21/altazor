Digital Concepts

The Solar System, Aligned. All eight planets lined up on one side of the Sun. Press "Make to scale" and both sizes and distances become true — the planets collapse to correctly spaced dots. Zoom into any planet for its shape and its numbers: area, mass, moons, what it is made of, when it was discovered and when a probe first reached it. Physical data from NASA's planetary fact sheets; moon counts as recognized in 2026.

Earth's Orbit as a Sine Wave Through Time. Earth circles the Sun once a year; add a time axis and the circle becomes a helix. Swing the view from the pole, where you see the orbit, to the side, where Earth just bobs up and down and its trace stretches back through the years as a sine wave. Sliders for the view angle, the time scale, how much history to keep, and the speed.

Mexico

Roads Around El Terrero, Namiquipa. The towns of the Namiquipa valley in Chihuahua and the municipal seats around them, each at its real coordinates and joined only where a road exists, with the driving distance along every link. Population from the 2020 census, distances routed over OpenStreetMap. The page is in Spanish.

USA

Every State by Population. All fifty states in order, largest to smallest, with each one's share of the country and its change since the 2020 census, from Census Bureau estimates.

Most Populous Cities in the United States. The twenty largest cities in 2025: population, share of the country, and how much each has grown or shrunk since the 2020 census, from Census Bureau estimates.

Demography

World's Most Populous Countries. The twenty largest countries in 2026: population, share of the world total, and births and deaths per day, from the United Nations projections.
