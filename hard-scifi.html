Hard Science Fiction

No book under the cursor

The books that keep the physics honest, from Verne's ballistics forward, colored by era. A click on the band of a decade enters it, and a book under the cursor shows its cover. Ctrl (or ⌘) with the wheel zooms; the wheel alone scrolls inside the frame and dragging pans the view.

Covers come from Open Library and are loaded from its servers for identification only. What counts as hard is argued about; every book here is named by the survey of the subgenre, the SF Encyclopedia or the SFWA classics list. The term itself was coined by P. Schuyler Miller in Astounding in 1957.
