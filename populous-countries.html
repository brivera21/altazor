World's Most Populous Countries

The twenty largest countries by population, with the share of the world each one holds and how many people are born and die in each per day. The green figure after each population is that country's lead: how many more people it has than the country one rank below it, so India's is its margin over China and the last row's is Tanzania's over Thailand, which sits just off this list. The paired bars run on one scale across every country, so their lengths compare between rows as well as within one. The first ten alone hold 56.6 percent of everyone alive; all twenty hold 69.3 percent.

Notes

Population figures are United Nations projections for 2026 under the medium variant, the middle of the range the UN publishes. Daily figures are the projected births and deaths for the whole year divided by 365.25, so they are an average day rather than any particular one. Where the lower bar is longer, deaths outnumber births: that is China, Russia, Japan and Germany. All four can still grow through migration, which these columns do not count. Flags are served by FlagCDN.
