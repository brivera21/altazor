Notes

Every country and area the United Nations counts separately, 237 of them, from India down to the Vatican. Population figures are projections for 2026 under the medium variant, the middle of the range the UN publishes. Daily figures are the projected births and deaths for the whole year divided by 365.25, so they describe an average day rather than any particular one; where that average is below one, the cell says so instead of showing zero. The ten largest hold 56.6 percent of the world between them. In 47 of these 237 places the lower bar is longer, meaning deaths outnumber births; all of them can still grow through migration, which these columns do not count. The rows add to 8,299,528,720, which is 99.99 percent of the UN's own world figure. The remainder is a gap in the source rather than a missing country: the UN's world record is larger than the sum of the places it lists. Flags are served by FlagCDN.
