Notes

Population figures are United Nations projections for 2026 under the medium variant, the middle of the range the UN publishes. Daily figures are the projected births and deaths for the whole year divided by 365.25, so they are an average day rather than any particular one. Only China and Russia are shrinking on their own births and deaths; both can still grow through migration, which these columns do not count. Flags are served by FlagCDN.
