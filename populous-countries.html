Notes

Population figures are United Nations projections for 2026 under the medium variant, the middle of the range the UN publishes. Daily figures are the projected births and deaths for the whole year divided by 365.25, so they are an average day rather than any particular one. China, Russia, Japan and Germany are the four here whose deaths outnumber their births; all four can still grow through migration, which these columns do not count. Flags are served by FlagCDN.
