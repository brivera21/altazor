The Universe

Hover a segment

The top bar is everything there is, by present-day energy content. Ordinary matter, the thin amber sliver, opens below into where its atoms actually sit. A segment under the cursor fills the card. Photons of the microwave background add about five thousandths of a percent and the known neutrino mass roughly a tenth of a percent; both are too thin to draw.
