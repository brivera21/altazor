How to read this

All fifty states from largest to smallest, with each one's share of the country and its change since the 2020 census, shaded on the map and drawn as bars, growth right and decline left. 43 states have grown since 2020 and 7 have lost people.

Sources

Notes

The green figure after a population is how many more people that state has than the one ranked below it. Wyoming has none because nothing is below it. The 2020 shares are of the April 1, 2020 estimates base for the fifty states and the District together (331,516,113); the ranks stay those of 2025.

Populations are Census Bureau estimates for July 1, 2025, and the comparison year is the April 1, 2020 estimates base, which is why the 2020 figures differ slightly from the published census counts. Shares are of the national total including the District of Columbia. Texas added 2,560,323 people since 2020, more than the entire population of any of the twelve smallest states. The state flags are drawn from the us-state-flags package and baked into the page; the District's flag is drawn by hand.
