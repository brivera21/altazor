Hominins

A group under the cursor lands here

The human tribe from the chimpanzee split to the present: the australopiths, the robust Paranthropus side branch, and every named branch of Homo down to the three that overlapped last, with fossil date ranges beside each species (Ma, millions of years ago; ka, thousands). Polytomies mark relationships the fossils leave unresolved, and X. A node under the cursor fills the card; a click pins it, and a second click lets go. Each species carries the photograph from its Wikipedia article, fetched at view time.
