Hugo and Nebula Winners

Hover over a book

Every Best Novel winner of both awards on one timeline. A book that won both appears once, in green. Click the band of a decade to enter it and see each book with its year; hover over a book to see its cover. Ctrl (or ⌘) + wheel zooms; the wheel alone scrolls inside the frame and dragging pans the view.

Covers come from Open Library and are loaded from its servers for identification only. The Hugo year is the year the award was presented; the Nebula year is the award's own label. The 2026 Hugo is presented on August 30, 2026.
