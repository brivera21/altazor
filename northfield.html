Northfield

A mark under the cursor lands here

The real city and its ground: terrain, woods, water, counties and the city limits in green dashes, each a layer to switch. The slider runs from 1492: the nations' homelands first, then the founding, the turns, the losses and the migrations that filled it. These nations still exist; Native Land Digital maps their territories with their input.
