Candidate moves are Alexander Kotov's term, from Think Like a Grandmaster, for the few moves in a position worth calculating: listed before any is calculated, each line followed once, the ends compared. The small version belongs in a player's first games, checks, captures and threats for both sides on every move; the full list belongs to the critical moments.
