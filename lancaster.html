Lancaster

A mark under the cursor lands here

The ground itself: relief shaded live from the AWS Terrain Tiles, woods and water from the USGS land cover, county lines from the Census files. Each chip turns a layer on or off; a mark under the cursor fills the card and a click pins it.

The slider runs from 1492: first the peoples whose ground this was, as colored patches approximating documented homelands, then the founding, the turns and the losses year by year. The city's circle grows with its census once it passes ten thousand, green through yellow and orange to red. Golden arrows are the migrations that filled it, joining regions rather than exact places. Mortarboards are colleges, each from the year it opened.
