Palme d'Or Winners

The top prize at Cannes, from the Grand Prix years through the Palme d'Or, 1946 to 2025, grouped into seven festival eras. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click an era band to zoom into it, hover a film to see its poster.

Years are festival years; before 1955 the prize was the Grand Prix du Festival. Gaps (1948, 1950, 1968, 2020) are years with no festival or no award. Posters are loaded at view time from Wikipedia's public API for identification and are not stored on this site; a few may fail to resolve.
